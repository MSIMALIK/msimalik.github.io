Supervision

Developing independent researchers.

I supervise research in explainable and trustworthy AI, multilingual NLP, low-resource language technology and multimodal learning. My supervision record includes two PhD theses, 22 master’s theses and 40 bachelor’s final-year projects.

Current supervised research includes explainable text detoxification for Nastaliq Urdu, SHAP and LIME explanations for right-to-left languages, multimodal hate-speech detection and social-media analysis for mental-health research. Related work explores knowledge-infused language models for violence-incitation detection.

Meet the researchers at MMX Lab. If you are interested in a master’s or doctoral research project, please send a concise message describing your interests and the question you would like to investigate.
