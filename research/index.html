Research vision

Explainable AI
across languages
and modalities

My research focuses on the intersection of cross-lingual multimodal deep learning, knowledge infusion, and AI explainability. I develop interpretable transformer architectures designed for low-resource languages and high-impact social NLP tasks with a primary emphasis on complex scripts like Nastaliq Urdu. By integrating external knowledge and analyzing visual-linguistic representations, my goal is to advance model interpretability and ensure transparent decision-making in socially sensitive applications.

01

Research strand

Explainable & trustworthy AI

Understanding how a model reaches its decision.

I study explanations for language and machine-learning models through LIME, SHAP, Grad-CAM, attention analysis and interpretable representations. My work also uses abstract meaning representation graphs and pattern structures to examine threatening language.

Core question
How can model decisions be examined and explained in ways that are useful to the people who rely on them?
Selected papersAMR graphs and pattern structures · 2025 ↗DeepThreatExplainer · 2024 ↗
02

Research strand

Multilingual & low-resource NLP

Language technology that works beyond English.

My research investigates cross-lingual transfer, multilingual learning and contextual representations for under-resourced languages. A particular focus is Nastaliq Urdu: detecting hate speech, identifying target communities, and modelling threatening, offensive and hope-related expressions.

Core question
Which representations and transfer strategies support reliable language understanding when labelled resources are limited?
Selected papersNastaliq Urdu hate speech · 2024 ↗Multilingual threatening text · 2023 ↗Multilingual hope speech · 2023 ↗
03

Research strand

Large Language Models

Adapting language models for low-resource languages.

My current research explores large language models through fine-tuning, prompt engineering and knowledge infusion, with particular attention to low-resource languages and Nastaliq Urdu. Current supervised work includes explainable text detoxification. This direction builds on my published research using fine-tuned transformer models for threatening content, propaganda and multilingual hope-speech detection.

Core question
How can large language models be adapted to low-resource languages while preserving meaning, reducing harmful content and making their outputs easier to evaluate and explain?
Related transformer publications Urdu-BERT for threatening content · 2023 ↗ RoBERTa for multilingual hope speech · 2023 ↗ BERT for propaganda detection · 2023 ↗
04

Research strand

Multimodal & applied AI

Models connected to practical decisions.

My applied work covers infrastructure and human damage assessment, electronic-product defect identification, review helpfulness, fraud detection and medical-query classification. Current supervised directions combine language and multimodal learning for mental-health signals and implicit or explicit hate speech.

Core question
How can language, contextual and cross-modal signals support useful and explainable systems for real-world tasks?
