Research directions

AI with context and a reason

01

Explainable & trustworthy AI

Grad-CAM, SHAP & attention Neuro-symbolic reasoning Interpretable deep learning AMR & pattern structures

Interpretability as a design requirement. Combining post-hoc explanations, attention analysis and structured representations to make model decisions easier to understand.

02

Multilingual & low-resource AI

Nastaliq Urdu NLP Cross-lingual transfer Multilingual transformers Contextual embeddings

Language technologies for under-represented languages, studying how scripts, contextual representations and transfer learning affect language understanding and evaluation.

03

Large Language Models

LLM fine-tuning Prompt engineering Knowledge infusion Explainable text detoxification

Adapting language models for low-resource understanding and generation, with current research on explainable text detoxification and preserving meaning while reducing harmful content.

04

Multimodal & applied AI

Vision–language models Cross-modal learning Multimodal classification Multimodal hate-speech detection

Bringing language and visual signals together for socially relevant tasks, including hate-speech detection, mental-health signals and damage assessment.
