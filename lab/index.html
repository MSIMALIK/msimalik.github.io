What we work on

Three directions.
One shared question.

How do we build AI whose behaviour we can understand across languages, modalities and applications?

Explainable AI

We treat interpretability as a design requirement rather than an afterthought. Our work spans post-hoc methods such as LIME, SHAP and attention analysis, and architectures that are explainable by construction, including neuro-symbolic approaches using abstract meaning representation and pattern structures.

LIME & SHAPAttention analysisNeuro-symbolic AI

Questions & methods

  • Post-hoc model explanations
  • Abstract meaning representation
  • Pattern structures
  • Explainable-by-construction architectures

Multilingual and low-resource language technology

Most of what the field knows about model behaviour was learned on English. We study what changes when it does not hold: cross-lingual transfer, pretrained models meeting under-represented scripts such as Nastaliq Urdu, and whether standard evaluation protocols stay valid once the language changes.

Cross-lingual transferNastaliq UrduEvaluation

Questions & methods

  • Learning across languages
  • Under-represented scripts
  • Pretrained language models
  • Evaluation across language settings

Multimodal and applied AI

We work on problems with a real cost attached - harmful content and hate speech detection, mental-health signals, damage assessment from social media, product-defect identification. Several of these were delivered as working prototypes under external grants, built by supervised student teams.
