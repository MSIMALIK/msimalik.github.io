Profile

Associate Professor of Computer Science at Pak-Austria Fachhochschule: Institute of Applied Sciences and Technology, Pakistan. Member of the Multimodal and Multilingual AI group at PAF-IAST.

Research interests include multilingual NLP, low-resource languages, social media analytics, multimodal AI, large language models, and explainable and trustworthy AI.
